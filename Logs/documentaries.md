## documenting everyday life

#### thinking about:
- meticulous attention paid to the unfolding of everyday life
- relinquishing the pretence of objectivity historically ascribed to the omnipresent, authorial documentarian
- subjectivity that inscribes meaning on everyday scenes
- the deliberate and continual interruption of the narrator
- self-reflexivity: the act of documenting or watching becomes the film
- unclear boundary between exploitation and empathy - how the relationship between subject and filmmaker comes into focus.
- first-person experiences of the world - close-up, cluttered, "raw" shots filmed on the go
- vulnerability of subject in an intimate setting, where the subject may be the filmmaker
- video diaries
